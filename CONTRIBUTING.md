# Contributing

Help keep the collection useful and accessible.

## Suggest a resource

Provide:

- Title and official publisher/project.
- Direct source URL.
- A brief original summary of what it provides.
- Suggested learner/practitioner audience.
- Category and practical use.
- Free, limited-free, or paid status.
- Account, eligibility, hardware, operating-system, or licensing requirements.
- Current price, currency, billing period, and source for paid items.
- Date checked and any uncertainty about access, maintenance, or publication status.

## Review criteria

Prefer freely accessible primary material, practical teaching value, clear authorship, and relevant OT/ICS content. Keep normative standards distinct from educational overviews, and temporary trials distinct from permanent free tiers. Vendor resources are welcome when their educational value is clear.

Avoid pirated copies, unofficial standards mirrors, affiliate-only recommendations, unexplained link dumps, and expired promotions. Check licensing before proposing redistribution.

## Maintain the catalog

Update the structured entry and its Markdown card together. Preserve IDs, remove duplicate entries, flag drafts and older material, and record meaningful price/access changes. Mark dead resources as archived and document replacements.

See LICENSE.md for the proposed scope of catalog, utility-code and brand licenses. Third-party resources retain their own terms.


## Repository checks

Edit resources.json, then run the commands in CLAUDE.md (including `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests`). Do not hand-edit the generated README. Preserve stable IDs and access limitations.
